Five small steps. That's all.

1 Download

⬇️
STEP 1

Download NUNU

Get the NUNU folder on your computer.

⬇ Download NUNU
After downloading, unzip the file.
🌐
STEP 2

Open Chrome Extensions

Paste this into Chrome's address bar.

chrome://extensions
Then press Enter.
🛠️
STEP 3

Turn on Developer mode

Look at the top-right corner.

Extensions
Developer mode
Make sure the switch is ON.
📂
STEP 4

Click “Load unpacked”

Then choose the NUNU folder you just unzipped.

Load unpacked
📁 nunu-ai-task-notifier-main
Pick the folder that contains manifest.json.
